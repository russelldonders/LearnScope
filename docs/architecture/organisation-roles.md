# Organisation roles and learner role alignment

Status: proposed contract for the multi-context LMS programme.

## Separate records

The learner controls their personal current role: title, narrative, dates,
skills, development and visibility. The organisation controls reusable role
profiles and employee role assignments. Neither side edits the other's record.

An organisation role profile defines a versioned set of:

- official title and role code;
- required and recommended skills with target levels;
- mandatory and recommended training;
- pathways, renewal rules and policy references; and
- organisational metadata such as function or grade.

An employee role assignment states that an organisation work profile occupies a
specific role-profile version for effective dates, optionally with manager,
department and explicit exceptions.

## Role alignment

A role alignment is a consent-based association between a learner's personal
role and an organisation role assignment. It exposes only the shared subset:
organisation requirements in one direction and learner-selected responses in
the other.

A requirement response can be not shared, acknowledged, in progress, supported
by selected evidence, awaiting review, satisfied, waived or expired. The
organisation determines satisfaction for its requirement; that decision does
not alter the learner's personal proficiency history.

Organisation changes are proposed to the learner. The learner may update an
existing personal role, create another role or leave their record unchanged.
Role-profile versions and effective dates preserve historical accuracy.
